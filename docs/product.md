# Product Strategy: Privacy Response Platform

## Target users

- high-profile individuals and families in RU/CIS markets,
- legal teams handling defamation/doxxing/privacy exposure,
- executive protection and reputation operations teams,
- managed privacy service operators.

## Positioning

This is an operations platform, not a single-click broker opt-out utility.

Core positioning:
- **Yandex-first discovery and deindexation logic** for Russian-speaking internet ecosystems.
- **Identity resolution quality** over noisy keyword scraping.
- **Source graph intelligence** to collapse mirrors and republish chains.
- **Legal-tech workflow support** with evidence integrity.
- **Continuous defense** against reappearance.

## Main workflows

1. Open case, build canonical person profile and identifiers.
2. Run discovery tasks with source-specific query strategies.
3. Resolve and cluster matches into source graph nodes.
4. Score risk and prioritize actions.
5. Launch removal/deindex/legal actions with traceability.
6. Store evidence snapshots and legal artifacts.
7. Monitor continuously and raise alerts on reappearance.

## Why RU/CIS and Yandex-first

- Data exposure dynamics and indexing behavior differ from US-centric assumptions.
- Yandex, local forums, social platforms, and mirrored repost networks require localized search heuristics.
- Legal processes, document templates, and compliance language vary by jurisdiction.
