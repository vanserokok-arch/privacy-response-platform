# Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Foundation (current)

- Monorepo scaffold with API, web, workers, shared packages, docs.
- Core entities and migrations.
- Baseline case/identifier/source APIs.
- Operations console shell.

## Stage 2 — Discovery + Identity

- Identity resolution pipeline with probabilistic matching and confidence calibration.
- Yandex-first query templates and parsers.
- Playwright-backed acquisition for dynamic sources.
- Source normalization and canonicalization.

## Stage 3 — Risk + Action Orchestration

- Risk scoring engine with explainable factors.
- Action routing policy across site owner / host / index / legal channels.
- Retry and SLA-aware orchestration.
- Human-in-the-loop review queues.

## Stage 4 — Legal + Monitoring

- Jurisdiction-aware legal document generator and workflow states.
- Evidence chain-of-custody metadata.
- Continuous monitoring rules and reappearance detection.
- Alerting and escalation flows.

## Stage 5 — Hardening / Enterprise / Scale

- RBAC, tenancy, policy controls, audit logging.
- Encryption-at-rest and key management.
- Observability (metrics, tracing, SLOs).
- Queue partitioning and horizontal scaling.
- Compliance and security hardening.
